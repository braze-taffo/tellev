## 1.7.1.7: Choose your interface

The new DSH style offers a simpler chat layout, composer and session drawer. Classic remains the default, so you can choose the look you prefer.

<!-- where -->
- Settings → Appearance → Interface style

<!-- steps -->
1. Select Classic or DSH. The interface changes immediately and your choice is saved automatically.
2. Both styles support Light, Dark and Follow system.
3. Your existing characters, world books and conversations remain available in either style.

## The DSH session drawer

Find and manage the selected character's conversations from the DSH chat screen.

<!-- where -->
- Choose DSH, open Chat and select a character
- The Sessions button in the chat toolbar

<!-- steps -->
1. Open Sessions, then tap a conversation title to switch. Use New chat to start another conversation.
2. Tap the search icon and enter part of a conversation title. Expand the remaining sessions when needed.
3. Deleting a session asks for confirmation. Back closes the drawer before leaving the conversation.

## Drafts and card script compatibility

Unsent text and images stay available when you visit Settings to change the interface and return to Chat. Special closing tags inside some card scripts are also handled correctly.

<!-- where -->
- The chat composer and pending images
- Settings → About → What is new

<!-- steps -->
1. Write a draft or attach an image, visit Settings to change the interface, then return to keep editing or send it.
2. Changing styles during generation does not start another request. Use Stop beside the composer to end the response.
3. This guide appears on the first launch after upgrading. Dismiss it once; you can reopen it from Settings → About whenever you need it.
